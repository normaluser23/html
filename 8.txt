$ node eventloop.js
1. Start of script (synchronous)
2. End of script (synchronous)
3. process.nextTick callback (runs before other microtasks)
4. Promise.then callback (microtask)
5. setTimeout callback (macrotask, runs after delay)
6. setImmediate callback (runs in the check phase)
